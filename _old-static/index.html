05 — FAQ

Questions?
Fair.

Can't find yours? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Which cards do you support?

RuPay, Mastercard and Visa credit cards from SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, YES BANK and other supported banks. Eligibility depends on the issuing bank.

How much does it cost?

Charges start at 1.6%. The exact rate depends on your card, bank and amount — we confirm it with you before you pay, so there are no surprises.

How fast do I get the money?

With instant mode (swipe or QR scan), the transaction is processed on the spot and the amount is sent to your account right after.

Will you ask for my OTP or PIN?

Never. Nobody from EKAM Group will ever ask for your card PIN, CVV or OTP. If someone does, it isn't us.

Can I book flights through you too?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp us with your route and dates and we'll get you options and fares.
